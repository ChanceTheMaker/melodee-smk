Beta status and current limits

This dual-core beta runs paired FM6 and Prophet voices on the second core, with serial fallback. Pro testing confirmed playing, project A-D and general-preset persistence, and 44.1 kHz PCM16 USB capture. Four-note stress tests kept USB capture flowing without worker faults, but recorded missed render deadlines and protective voice shedding. Other engines remain on the first core; this is not a promise of double performance.

Bluetooth and device sample uploads are not available. Elite hardware, USB playback/PCM24, physical MIDI expression and longer audio tests remain on the follow-up checklist.

Meet your new custom SMK-37 controls...

Choose a group to highlight the controls and learn what they do.

Knobs

KNOB BANK off
Knob 1: master volume. Knob 2: tempo / SELECT. Knob 3: factory presets. Knob 4: track / ALGORITHM. Knobs 5–8: the current page’s K1–K4 controls.
KNOB BANK on
Knobs 1–4: attack, decay, sustain and release. Knobs 5–8: distortion, chorus, delay and reverb.
Turn clockwise
Increase the value or move forward through the choices.

Sound pages

PATCH / HOME
Return to Melodee’s native home page.
BT / EDIT
Open the synth editing pages. This button controls Edit; Bluetooth is not enabled.
FX / GLOBE
Open the native effects or global controls.
ARP / NOTE REPEAT
Open the native arpeggiator or LFO page.
SCALE / CHORD
Open the native scale or envelope page. Custom firmware changes the original button labels’ functions.

Play & record

SEQ
Open Melodee’s native sequencer pages.
PLAY / REC
Start or stop playback; use REC for the native recording workflow.
Four tracks
Select a track with knob 4 in bank 1. Use Studio for a wider view of the pattern and track controls.
Keep your work
Save a project before powering off. First-time device saves require storage setup.

Keys & wheels

37 KEYS
Play the selected track with velocity. The starting range is C3–C6; octave buttons shift it.
PITCH / MOD
Bend the pitch or apply modulation to the current sound.
SUSTAIN
Connect a pedal to hold notes after releasing the keys.
Native shortcuts
FM-1 key-layer shortcuts use the inner F3–G5 range. The browser simulator’s question-mark button opens its tutorial.

Pads & faders

16 PADS
Play the drum track with velocity. The pads use one sixteen-pad bank.
FADER BANK off
Faders 1–4 set the levels of tracks 1–4.
FADER BANK on
Faders 1–4 set the selected track’s attack, decay, sustain and release.
Pad lights
Physical hits use the corresponding pad’s feedback light. The new brightness levels are part of the b1 device checks.

Save & arrange

PARA / SAVE
Open Melodee’s native save interface for projects and presets.
First-time storage
Use the separate backup/setup tool after installing b1, then restart. Initialization replaces prior saved music and sample data only after a verified backup and confirmation.
Projects & presets
Melodee keeps project banks, general presets, native FM6/CZ/Prophet presets, favorites and settings. Device persistence is awaiting b1 validation.
Browser export
Studio and Simulator share a browser session. Export it to keep an independent backup; imports require the matching runtime version.

05 / FIRST NOTES

Start in the browser.

  1. Open Studio or Simulator. If audio is blocked, play a key or use Enable audio.
  2. Play with your mouse, touchscreen or computer keyboard. Simulator numbers 1–8 play the top pads; Shift+1–8 play the bottom pads.
  3. Choose Enable USB-MIDI input to play from a connected controller.
  4. Export your session to keep a backup outside this browser. Device projects and browser sessions are stored separately.
