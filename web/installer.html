Prepare your installation

  1. Export any important projects and presets from your current firmware. Other firmware’s device saves are not automatically compatible with Melodee.
  2. Close Studio, Simulator and other MIDI apps. Use current desktop Chrome or Edge with a direct USB data connection.
  3. Select your model and the firmware currently on your SMK. Preparation checks the files without connecting to your device.
  4. After preparation, confirm the notice and click Install. Allow MIDI/SysEx access, and leave power and USB connected throughout.

Automatically detects stock firmware, an active loader, or Felucca / Sloop / Melodee SMK.

What’s in this build

  • CPU1 rendering for paired FM6 / Prophet voices, with automatic single-core fallback
  • Native Melodee synthesis and 240 × 240 interface
  • SMK piano, pads, encoders, faders, wheels and sustain mappings
  • USB MIDI and a bounded RAM update loader
  • Projects, presets, favorites and settings with guarded storage setup
  • TRS MIDI output, sustain, pitch/mod and internal clock/Start/Stop
  • 44.1 kHz USB audio: stereo playback and four track captures

Still being integrated

User sample uploads and Bluetooth remain unavailable. The new device storage, TRS and USB audio integration needs the b1 hardware checks. Browser sessions save separately in Studio and Simulator.

Pro and Elite package generation and loader behavior pass offline tests. The earlier dev1 build boots and plays on the tester’s SMK; b1’s new integrations still need device validation. The stock Elite loader may require a device confirmation before installation continues. Physical Elite handoff testing remains pending.

First-time device saves

After installing b1, open storage backup and setup. Download the verified backup before initializing, then restart the SMK. Setup is separate from firmware installation.

Protected data

The custom RAM updater validates the flash profile and calibration, verifies the complete package before erasing, and limits writes to the application region. The stock route uses the matching official 016 loader. Neither route is a backup of your existing music.
